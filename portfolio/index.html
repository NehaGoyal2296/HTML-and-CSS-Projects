01 / About

A little about my journey.

I’m Neha, and I created this portfolio to bring my HTML and CSS coursework together in one place. Building each project has helped me understand how a webpage grows from a simple idea into something people can explore in a browser.

I’m still learning, and that is part of the fun. These projects show the concepts I’ve practised so far—from organising content and styling layouts to adding navigation, images, forms, and responsive details.
